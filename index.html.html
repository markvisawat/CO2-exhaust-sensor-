CarbonTrack™

The Digital Odometer for Carbon Emissions

Continuous lifetime CO₂ monitoring for petrol and diesel vehicles.

The Problem

❌ Emissions measured during short testing periods
❌ Manufacturers rely on estimated emissions data
❌ No lifetime vehicle emissions tracking
❌ Limited real-world performance insights

The Solution

Meet CarbonTrack™

An inline CO₂ exhaust sensor designed for petrol and diesel vehicles. CarbonTrack continuously measures and stores emissions data throughout the entire life of the vehicle. Data can be accessed remotely or during routine inspections such as WOF checks.

  • Continuous CO₂ Monitoring
  • Lifetime Data Collection
  • Remote Access
  • WOF Compatible
  • Real-World Driving Data
