CarbonTrack™

The Digital Odometer for Carbon Emissions

Continuous lifetime CO₂ monitoring for petrol and diesel vehicles.

The Problem

❌ Current testing captures only a short snapshot of emissions.
❌ Manufacturers rely on estimated environmental performance.
❌ No practical system tracks lifetime CO₂ emissions.
❌ Valuable real-world data is lost.

The Solution

Meet CarbonTrack™

An inline exhaust sensor that continuously measures and stores lifetime CO₂ emissions data. Information can be accessed remotely or during WOF inspections, helping manufacturers improve future vehicle designs.

  • Continuous monitoring
  • Lifetime data storage
  • Remote access
  • WOF compatible
  • Real-world emissions intelligence
